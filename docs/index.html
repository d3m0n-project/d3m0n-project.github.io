Community Friendly

d3m0n devices are made to be enhanced by its users

  • Privacy

    Privacy is our foundation. Every device is designed to minimize data collection, maximize transparency, and give you complete control over how your hardware and software operate.

  • Open Hardware

    Our hardware is built to be opened, modified, repaired, and improved. Whether you're experimenting, upgrading, or creating something entirely new, the platform is yours to customize.

  • Documentation

    From getting started to advanced modifications, our documentation includes guides, schematics, and technical references to help you understand every aspect of your hardware.

  • Community

    We're building a community of makers, hackers, developers, and privacy advocates who collaborate, share knowledge, contribute improvements, and inspire new ideas.

  • Freedom

    Technology should belong to its owner. Avoid vendor lock-in, install the software you choose, modify your devices freely, and keep full control over your digital experience.
